## Acceptance criteria

- The report lists every check with result, evidence (command and output excerpt) and the consequence for the design. Example: "change base URL to …", or "keep ADR-0004 / switch to LiteLLM".
- If any of checks 1–6 fails in a way that blocks virtual keys or budgets, include a LiteLLM variant of `deploy/gateway/` and the Compose service as a proposal. Do not switch the default without review.
- Proposed contract or ADR changes are listed in the report only; the files themselves are not edited in this task.
- Provider keys are never committed; scripts read them from the environment.

## Constraints

Time-box: 4 days. Use the smallest model of each provider. Total LLM spend under 2 USD. The decision of check 16 is recorded as a dated amendment in `adr/0002` after review.
